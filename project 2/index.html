Recipe for Chicken Biryani

About the Chicken Biryani

Chicken Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with basmati rice, chicken, and a mixture of spices. It is a rich and flavorful dish that is often served at special occasions and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Rice
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Green Chillies
  • Spices
  • Curd
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Masala
  • GaramMasala
  • BiryaniMasala
  • Water

Steps How to make Chicken Biryani

  1. Wash and Soak the Rice in water for 30minutes
  2. Cut the Chicken into pieces
  3. Marinate the Chicken with Curd, Ginger, Garlic, Green Chillies, Spices, Masala, GaramMasala, BiryaniMasala
  4. Heat oil in a pan
  5. Add the marinated Chicken to the pan
  6. Cook the Chicken for 15-20 minutes
  7. Add the soaked Rice to the pan
  8. Add water
  9. Cook the Rice for 15-20 minutes
  10. Serve hot

Tips for making the best Chicken Biryani

  • Use good quality Basmati rice
  • Use fresh chicken
  • Use good quality spices
  • Do not overcook the chicken
  • Do not overcook the rice
  • Serve hot

Result

Delicious Chicken Biryani is ready to serve.
